What We Do

Applied Sciences is Providence's research and development engine. It turns raw anomalous science into patentable, dependable products for every other division - from the shielding in a Bedrock contract to the supplement on a store shelf - and is publicly recognized for setting the industry benchmark on safe handling of Rift-derived materials.

Our research charter is reviewed annually by an independent standards board, and our long-term wellness studies - conducted in partnership with Continuity Assurance and Providence Health - help us understand how communities live safely alongside anomalous conditions over time.

Innovation Governance

A dedicated internal steering group coordinates Applied Sciences' longest-horizon work, aligning priorities directly with founder and CEO Julian Ashworth's vision for the company's next chapter.

This group works closely with Continuity Assurance, Providence Health, and Community Solutions to make sure the data behind our products and programs is put to the best possible use - for policyholders, patients, and residents alike.

Executive Profile

The Architect of Continuity

One founder's conviction that the future of anomalous science would be built, sold, and lived in - not filed away.

Julian Ashworth Founder & CEO

At a Glance

  • Title Founder, Chairman & CEO
  • Background Ex-Commission Materials Science
  • Tenure 2012 - Present
  • Reports To Board of Directors

A former Commission materials engineer, Julian Ashworth left the organization during the Culling, convinced that anomalous science's future was commercial, not classified. He saw that while governments contained and militaries weaponized, no one was selling the public solutions.

Ashworth incorporated Providence in Nevada, building the Continuity Spire on cheap desert land far from regulatory scrutiny. Under his guidance, Providence grew from selling home detection kits into a trillion-dollar entity dictating global infrastructure.
